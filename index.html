How it works

Three moves, then you are live

Pick a look, press Start, and choose Stream Live as the camera in whatever app you are already using.

  1. Install and sign up. Run the installer, open Stream Live, create an account in the app. It works the moment it is created.
  2. Pick a look and a voice. Choose a style, then a face or voice reference. Video, voice, or both together.
  3. Select it in your app. In the call or streaming app, choose Stream Live Camera and Stream Live Microphone — on Windows the audio one may be listed as CABLE Input or VB-Audio Virtual Cable, which is the same device — on a Mac they are named OBS Virtual Camera and BlackHole 2ch. Then press Start in Stream Live. Your own camera stays off-air.
On a Mac? The short version of this page is the Mac install walk-through: approve the app once, allow camera and microphone, add the two free helpers, then point OBS at the Stream Live window.

Mac install

Getting Stream Live onto a Mac

Five steps, about three minutes. Do them in order; steps 1 and 2 are one-time, and steps 4 and 5 are what make a call app see the AI picture and hear your voice.

  1. Move the app into Applications. Double-click the .dmg you downloaded, drag Stream Live onto Applications, then eject the disk. Keep it in Applications — running it from the Downloads folder is what makes later updates fail.
  2. Clear the “damaged” warning, once. Stream Live is not signed with a paid Apple developer certificate, so the first launch can say “Stream Live is damaged and can’t be opened”. That is the missing signature talking, not a broken download — the file you got is complete. Two things to do: finish step 1 so the app really is in Applications, then open Terminal (Cmd + Space, type Terminal, press Return) and paste this line, then press Return:
    xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine "/Applications/Stream Live.app"
    Launch Stream Live normally after that — it stays cleared and you never do this again. If Terminal answers No such file, the app is not in Applications yet, so go back to step 1. If it asks for permission, run the same line with sudo in front of it and type your Mac login password — nothing appears on screen while you type it, which is normal.
  3. Allow the camera and the microphone. In System Settings → Privacy & Security, switch Stream Live on under both Camera and Microphone. Without them the preview stays black and no voice is cloned.
  4. Install the two free helpers. They do the job the Windows installer does with bundled drivers: one carries sound, one carries video.
    • BlackHole 2ch — a silent virtual speaker; your voice goes in here. Clicking it downloads the installer straight into your Downloads folder. It is free software and that link asks you for nothing — no sign-up, no email, no payment page. After installing it, log out and back in, and it appears under System Settings → Sound and in the app’s “Audio out” list. If the link ever fails, message us on Telegram and we will send you the file.
    • OBS Studio — turns the Stream Live window into a webcam.
  5. Put the AI feed into your call. On the stream screen, open the OBS window and turn Stream Live Microphone on, so your voice moves to BlackHole 2ch — or the cloned voice on the tabs where cloning is switched on. Then:
    1. In OBS: Sources → + → Window Capture → pick “Stream Live OBS”.
    2. In OBS: Controls → Start Virtual Camera.
    3. In Zoom, Teams, Meet or WhatsApp: Camera → OBS Virtual Camera, Microphone → BlackHole 2ch.
Wear headphones. Your voice now travels through BlackHole 2ch while your real microphone is still open, so loudspeakers send the call straight back into it and everyone hears echo. Do not add your own microphone as a second input in the call app either — your voice is already on that device, and two copies of it arrive as echo. OBS carries picture only, which is why the call app takes its microphone from BlackHole rather than from OBS.

macOS only accepts a system webcam from software Apple has approved with a special entitlement, so on a Mac the picture always travels through OBS. Everything else — the styles, portrait mode, the automatic microphone, your minute balance — works the same as on Windows.

Questions

Before you download, and after

The things people ask most. Anything not answered here reaches a person on Telegram in one tap.

Which file do I need?

Windows 10 or 11 takes the Windows installer — that is the one most people want. A Mac takes the Apple chip disk if About This Mac says Chip: Apple M…, and the Intel disk if it says Processor: Intel…. Everything is under Downloads.

What does it cost to try?

Nothing. Every new account carries one free minute of video, with no card and no trial period to cancel. It does not expire while you decide. After that, airtime is bought in minutes inside the app, by card or mobile money.

Does it work in my app?

Anywhere a camera and microphone can be chosen: OBS, Zoom, Microsoft Teams, Google Meet, WhatsApp Web and most browser-based calls. On Windows the installer adds both devices itself; on a Mac the two free helpers carry them, which is what the Mac walk-through covers.

Is the picture really live?

Yes. Nothing is rendered afterwards and nothing is uploaded to be processed — what you say is what goes out, and the avatar keeps your movement, your timing and your eye contact.

I am locked out of my account

If you reached this page from an email link, the link reached us — but Stream Live does not change a password through a web form, so there is nothing to fill in here.

  1. Open the app and use Plans to send your account email to the contact shown there.
  2. An administrator sets a new password and sends it back to you.
  3. If the app will not start, message on Telegram or reply to the email you signed up with — both reach the team.
Will my stream run out mid-call?

Airtime stops when your balance hits zero, and the call closes rather than running into debt. Add minutes before a long session: the app tells you the balance at all times.
